# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive participation from people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. We aim to keep project discussions and
reviews respectful, useful, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Offer specific, constructive feedback about the work rather than attacking
  contributors.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or
  confidential communications without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for others to participate.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation,
unwelcome sexual attention, doxxing, and repeated disruptive conduct are not
acceptable. This also includes hostile or demeaning language directed at a
person or group based on identity or background.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews,
and other project spaces where someone is representing or participating in
this project.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainer using a private contact
method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive
details in a public issue. Include relevant links and a concise description of
what happened; reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

If you cannot reach the maintainer or the concern involves GitHub platform
behavior, use GitHub's reporting or support channels.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer is responsible for responding to reports and
enforcing this policy. Depending on the situation, they may ask someone to
change or remove content, issue a warning, close a discussion or contribution,
or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Serious behavior may
result in immediate action without a prior warning.

Retaliation against someone for raising a concern in good faith is not
acceptable.
